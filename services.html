Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you might want to know before picking up the phone.

How quickly can you respond to storm damage?

In most cases within 24 hours, often the same day. We carry tarp and emergency dry-in materials on every truck. After we secure the roof, we'll document the damage with drone photography and stick with you through the entire insurance claim.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes, Guardian is fully insured for general liability and workers' compensation, and licensed to operate across Indiana and the southern Michigan border. Certificates available on request, and we encourage you to verify any contractor you hire.

What roofing brands do you install?

For shingles: Owens Corning, GAF, CertainTeed, and Malarkey. For metal: Drexel Metals, Decra, and McElroy. For commercial: Carlisle and Versico TPO/EPDM systems. We're certified with the manufacturers we install, which gives our customers access to extended warranty options most contractors can't offer.

Do you handle insurance claims?

We do. We don't act as your public adjuster, but we know how to document a roof properly, build a scope of loss the way insurers expect to see it, and meet with your adjuster on-site so nothing gets quietly denied.

Is financing available?

Yes. Guardian partners with Enhancify to offer flexible monthly payment plans, including 0% promotional options for qualified buyers. Most homeowners get a financing decision in minutes with no impact to their credit score.

What's the $1,000 upgrade about?

For a limited time, every full roof replacement comes with a $1,000 upgrade, typically applied toward premium shingles, an upgraded ridge ventilation system, or a gutter guard package. We'll walk you through the options at your estimate.
