Financing Questions

Good to know before you apply.

Does checking my options affect my credit?

No. Pre-qualification uses a soft credit pull, which has no impact on your credit score. A hard pull only happens if you choose to move forward and formally accept a loan offer.

Who is Enhancify?

Enhancify is a home-improvement financing platform that connects you with multiple lenders at once, so you can compare real offers in one place. Guardian partners with them so you're not stuck with a single bank's terms.

Can I finance gutters or siding too?

Yes. Financing isn't limited to roof replacements, it can cover gutters, gutter guards, siding, windows, and combined exterior projects.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

It depends on the lender and plan you choose, but many options allow you to pay off early with no penalty. We'll point out the terms before you sign anything.

Can I combine financing with the $1,000 upgrade?

Yes. The $1,000 upgrade applies to your full roof replacement regardless of whether you pay outright or finance. Mention it when you book your estimate.
