Quick Answers

Before you call.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. No fees, no obligation, no pressure. We come out, climb the roof, document everything, and send you a written estimate. You hire us or you don't, either way, the inspection report is yours to keep.

How quickly can you come out?

Most estimates are scheduled within 2–3 business days. Storm and emergency calls are handled same-day where possible. If you're in a hurry, just call 260-619-8026.

Do you charge for storm inspections?

No. Storm damage inspections are always free, including drone documentation and a written damage report you can share with your insurance company.

Do you work with my insurance company?

We've worked with every major carrier, State Farm, Allstate, Farmers, USAA, Liberty Mutual, and most regionals. We'll meet your adjuster on-site and document everything to insurer standards.

Where do you serve?

Fort Wayne and surrounding cities including Huntertown, Leo, Cedarville, New Haven, Auburn, Columbia City, Huntington, Ossian, Decatur, Angola, Kendalville, and across the Michigan border to Coldwater. Not sure if you're in our area? Just ask.
