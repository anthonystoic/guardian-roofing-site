Privacy Policy

How Guardian Roofing & Gutters collects, uses, and protects the information you share with us.

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

Guardian Roofing & Gutters ("Guardian," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this site or submitting a form, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us, your name, phone number, email, property address, ZIP code, and any details you share when you request a quote, book an inspection, or contact us.
  • Automatic information, basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, collected through standard web analytics and our hosting provider.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your quote, inspection, or service requests.
  • To contact you by phone, text message, or email about your project, appointment, or estimate.
  • To provide and improve our services and this website.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect our business.

Phone & Text (SMS) Consent

When you submit a form or provide your phone number, you consent to be contacted by Guardian by phone, text message, and email regarding your request and our services. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and reply HELP for help. Opting out of texts will not affect your ability to receive a quote by phone or email. We do not sell or share your phone number or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing.

Cookies & Analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies to keep the site working properly and understand how visitors use it. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some site features.

Third-Party Services

We use trusted third parties to operate our business, which may process limited information on our behalf, for example, our website host (Netlify), web analytics providers, and our financing partner (Enhancify) if you choose to apply for financing. These providers have their own privacy policies governing their use of your information.

How We Protect Your Information

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information you share. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

  • Opt out of text messages by replying STOP.
  • Request that we update or delete the contact information you've provided.
  • Decline to provide information, though this may limit our ability to serve you.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or your information? Contact us at 260-619-8026 or tyler@guardingthefort.com, Guardian Roofing & Gutters, Fort Wayne, Indiana.

This policy is provided as a general template and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel before launch to ensure it meets your specific obligations.
