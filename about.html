Meet the Owner

Meet Tyler.

Tyler grew up around Fort Wayne and started in the trades as a teenager. After years of working under contractors who cut corners and pressured customers, he launched Guardian to bring a fresh standard to a trade most homeowners dread.

As a young owner with a family of his own, Tyler runs Guardian the way he'd want a contractor to treat his own home. That means honest estimates, real crews, and the kind of communication you'd expect from a friend in the business. When you call Guardian, you're calling the owner, not a call center. When the crew shows up, Tyler is with them. And when the job is done, he's the one walking it with you.

Our Values

What we stand for.

Four values that show up on every job. They're the reason we get hired, and the reason our customers send their neighbors our way.

01, Honesty

A straight answer, every time.

If your roof has ten more years on it, we'll tell you. If a repair is smarter than a replacement, we'll tell you that too. The hard-sell roofing playbook is the one we threw out on day one.

02, Craft

Done right or done again.

Every flashing detail, every valley, every ridge. We don't move on from a job until it would pass inspection on Tyler's own house, because that's the standard you're paying for.

03, Communication

You'll always know what's next.

Text updates from start to finish. Crews that introduce themselves. A real human picking up the phone. You won't have to chase us, we'll be ahead of you the whole way.

04, Respect

Your home, your timeline.

Tarps on landscaping. Magnetic nail sweeps at end of shift. We treat your property like a neighbor's, because in Fort Wayne, you usually are one. Your home is left better than we found it.
