Accessibility Statement

We want every visitor, including people with disabilities, to be able to use this website and reach us easily.

Last updated: June 2026

Our Commitment

Guardian Roofing & Gutters is committed to ensuring our website is accessible to people of all abilities. We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA as a practical standard for usability and inclusion.

What We Do

  • Use clear, high-contrast text and a readable type system.
  • Provide descriptive alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Build with semantic HTML so the site works with screen readers and keyboards.
  • Support navigation by keyboard and on mobile devices.
  • Avoid relying on color alone to convey information.

Ongoing Effort

Accessibility is an ongoing process, not a one-time task. We continue to review and improve the site as we add content and features. Some older content or third-party tools may not yet fully meet every standard, and we work to address issues as we find them.

Need Help, or Found a Problem?

If you have trouble accessing any part of this website, or you'd like information in a different format, we want to hear from you, and we'll help you directly. Contact us:

  • Phone or text: 260-619-8026
  • Email: tyler@guardingthefort.com

Please let us know the page and the issue you ran into so we can fix it and assist you. You'll never need this website to get a quote, a phone call works just as well.
