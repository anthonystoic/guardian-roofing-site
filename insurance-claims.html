How It Works

Five steps from storm to new roof.

Insurance claims feel intimidating because most homeowners only file one in a lifetime. We do it every week. Here's exactly how the process goes when you call Guardian.

01, Free Inspection

We document everything.

We climb the roof, photograph hail bruising, wind creasing, and damaged flashing, and capture it all with drone imagery. You get a written damage report whether or not you file.

02, File the Claim

You call it in, we prep you.

You file with your carrier, we tell you exactly what to say and what to send. We never pose as a public adjuster; we make sure nothing gets overlooked.

03, Meet the Adjuster

We're on the roof with them.

We meet your insurance adjuster on-site, walk the damage together, and make sure the scope of loss reflects the real condition of your roof, not a quick drive-by estimate.

04, Approval & Scope

We build it to insurer standards.

Once approved, we put together a proper scope and material selection. You pick colors and upgrades; we handle the paperwork and the supplement if the first estimate falls short.

05, Install & Final Check

New roof, claim closed.

Certified crews install your new system, we submit the completion documents your insurer needs, and Tyler walks the finished job with you. You pay your deductible, nothing hidden.

★, Always

No pressure, no chasing.

If the damage doesn't justify a claim, we'll tell you. We'd rather earn a repair and your trust than push you into a claim you don't need.

Right After a Storm

What to do before you call anyone.

A few simple steps protect your home and your claim. Do what's safe from the ground, leave the roof to us.

Document the Damage

Photograph anything you can see safely, dented gutters, fallen branches, shingles in the yard, water spots inside.

Limit Interior Damage

If water's coming in, move valuables and put a bucket down. Don't climb up to tarp it yourself, call us.

Find Your Policy

Locate your homeowner's policy number and carrier phone line so you're ready when it's time to file.

Call Guardian First

Before you sign anything with a door-knocker, get a free, honest inspection from a local crew you can find next year.

Claim Questions

What homeowners ask us most.

Straight answers before you file.

Will filing a claim raise my rates?

A single weather-related claim (hail or wind) typically falls under "act of God" and is treated differently than an at-fault claim. Many carriers don't surcharge for it, but every policy is different, check with your agent. We'll never push you to file a claim that isn't worth it.

How much will it cost me out of pocket?

If your claim is approved, you're generally responsible only for your deductible. Insurance covers the approved scope of the replacement. We'll explain exactly what's covered before any work begins.

Do you charge for the storm inspection?

No. Storm and hail inspections are always free, including drone documentation and a written report you can hand directly to your insurance company.

My neighbor's roofer knocked on my door. Should I sign?

Be careful. Storm-chasing crews often have you sign a contract or "assignment of benefits" on the spot, then disappear. Get a free second opinion from a local, certified contractor first, and read anything before you sign it.

What if my claim gets denied or underpaid?

It happens, sometimes the first estimate misses damage. We document thoroughly, can request a re-inspection, and submit a supplement with proper photos and measurements when the scope falls short.

Which insurance companies have you worked with?

Every major carrier, State Farm, Allstate, Farmers, USAA, Liberty Mutual, American Family, Erie, and most regional insurers. We know how each likes documentation presented.
